  • V4: Working towards MVP solutions Work in progress

    High priority

    1. Test the timeline with AI analysis design with users to see if it helps them understand the case better, and to get feedback on the design itself.
    2. Understand where the case is in its lifecycle and tailor the information presented accordingly - e.g. if the case is still with the police, focus on the information that would be most relevant to a reviewing lawyer (e.g. proposed charges, evidence summary, AI analysis of strength of evidence); if the case has been charged, include more information about the defendant's history and the strength of the case against them to help advocates prepare for court.
    3. Test some loading time ideas and how to communicate that to users things have changed since they last looked at the case.
    4. Allow filtering by person on the charges and timeline to make it easier to navigate the information for cases with multiple defendants and/or multiple charges.
    5. Displaying an evaluation of whether all the materials in the case are present.

    Lower priority

    1. Add links to legal guidance and keep testing vague "strong"/"weak" tags with users.
    2. Confidence scores for AI suggestions - test different ways of doing this and the impact on user trust and reliance on the information.

    Last updated 17 March 2026
