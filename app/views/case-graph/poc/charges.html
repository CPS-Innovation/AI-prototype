Reason for alternative
If the court is not satisfied beyond reasonable doubt that defendant appropriated with dishonest intent to permanently deprive, they may still find her guilty of handling if she dishonestly received goods she knew or believed to be stolen.
Statutory definition
A person is guilty of handling stolen goods if, knowing or believing them to be stolen goods, he dishonestly receives the goods, or dishonestly undertakes or assists in their retention, removal, disposal or realisation by another person.(Theft Act 1968 s.22(1))
